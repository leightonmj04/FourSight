# FourSight Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

FourSight is a degree-planning tool that runs entirely in your web browser, as a Chrome extension. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it.

## The short version

FourSight does not collect, send, sell or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your own computer.

## What FourSight stores, and where

FourSight saves your degree plan so it is still there the next time you open it. This includes:

- the program you selected
- which semester each course is in, and any terms you added
- courses you marked complete, and courses you added, edited or deleted
- course results from a transcript you imported

This information is stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else, and there is no FourSight account or server.

## Transcripts

If you import a transcript (as a PDF file or pasted text), FourSight reads it inside your browser to find the courses on it. The file and its contents are never uploaded anywhere. FourSight keeps only the course results it needs for your plan, stored on your device as described above.

## What FourSight does not do

- It does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, UT EID or location.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies.
- It does not connect to the internet while you use it. All of its code, including the PDF reader, is included in the extension. The only exception is when you click a link in the Program notes window (such as a UT catalog page); that opens the website in a new tab, like any other link.
- It requests no special Chrome permissions and cannot read your other tabs or browsing.

## Deleting your data

Your data stays until you remove it. You can:

- use **Reset to suggested** in FourSight, with the "keep" boxes unchecked, to clear a plan and its imported transcript results, or
- remove the FourSight extension from Chrome, which deletes everything it stored.

## Not affiliated with UT Austin

FourSight is an independent student planning tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by The University of Texas at Austin, and it is not an official degree audit.

## Overview

Cockrell's 11 BS programs run from 122 hours (Computational) to 132 (Biomedical and Geosystems), all built on the same calculus, physics and mechanics foundation. Requirements come from the [2026–28 Undergraduate Catalog](https://catalog.utexas.edu/undergraduate/programs/). Prerequisites come from the 2025–26 course listings, because the 2026–27 course PDFs leave prerequisite text out. All courses need a C- or better, and "(co)" means a course can be taken in the same semester.

```text
Shared foundation prerequisite chain (→ = prerequisite of, (co) = may be taken concurrently)

M 408C → M 408D ─┬→ M 427J
                 ├→ M 427L
                 ├→ E M 306 ─┬→ E M 311M
                 │           └→ E M 319
                 └(co)→ PHY 303K + 105M ─┬→ PHY 303L + 105N
                                         └→ E M 306

RHE 306 → E S 333T
```

M 408D is the bottleneck: it unlocks M 427J, M 427L and E M 306, and must be done or in progress for PHY 303K and 303L. A student who finishes M 408C and 408D in the first year keeps every major's sophomore schedule open.

**Flowchart and approved elective lists:** the department posts degree plans, flowcharts and the current approved technical elective lists in a [Box folder](https://utexas.app.box.com/v/degree-plan-flow-charts) (shared with Computational Engineering).

## Biomedical Engineering (BSBiomedE)

132 hours in all four concentrations: a shared 96-hour degree core, 12 hours of concentration electives, and 24 hours of remaining core curriculum. Pick a concentration by the end of sophomore year; the advising office must approve technical courses before you register. Catalog pages: [Imaging and Instrumentation](https://catalog.utexas.edu/undergraduate/programs/biomedical-engineering-biomedical-imaging-instrumentation-bsbiomede/), [Cellular and Biomolecular](https://catalog.utexas.edu/undergraduate/programs/biomedical-engineering-cellular-biomolecular-engineering-bsbiomede/), [Computational](https://catalog.utexas.edu/undergraduate/programs/biomedical-engineering-computational-biomedical-engineering-bsbiomede/), [Molecular, Cellular, and Tissue Biomechanics](https://catalog.utexas.edu/undergraduate/programs/biomedical-engineering-molecular-cellular-tissue-biomechanics-bsbiomede/).

**Flowchart:** I could not find a public BME flowchart; the catalog's plan of study is the closest published sequence, and the catalog says current elective lists are on the BME advising office's website.

**Critical path (no slack):** M 408C → M 408D → M 427J → BME 313L → BME 343 → BME 349 → BME 370 → BME 371. That is eight courses for eight semesters, so a delay anywhere on it pushes graduation.

**Shared foundation courses required** (prerequisites in the Overview): M 408C, M 408D, M 427J, PHY 303K + 105M, PHY 303L + 105N, E S 333T, RHE 306. BME uses CH 401 and 402 instead of CH 301 and 302.

## Sources and caveats

This guide follows the 2026–28 catalog. Students may graduate under any catalog in effect while they were enrolled in Cockrell, so anyone who started before fall 2026 should check the catalog they are on.

**What to double-check:**

- **Prerequisites** come from the 2025–26 course listings, because the 2026–27 course PDFs omit prerequisite text. A few lines may have changed, most visibly the PGE prerequisites that still name courses Geosystems dropped and the new M E 466E capstone. The catalog's course search shows current prerequisites course by course.
- **Approved elective lists** are department documents. Chemical's list is the 2026–28 version; Petroleum and Geosystems lists are 2024–26; Mechanical's track courses come from its spring and fall 2026 offering sheets; the ASE/COE and CAEE lists sit in Box folders that would not open from here, so those sections list the catalog's course pools instead.
- **ECE free electives:** the department states 11 hours for 2026–28, while four concentration pages in the catalog still show 14.
- **Biomedical:** no public flowchart was found.
- Confirm any individual plan against the Interactive Degree Audit and the department advising office.
